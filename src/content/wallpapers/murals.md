A wall mural is a single picture — forest, city, abstract, map — printed in numbered drops that align on your wall. It is the right choice when you want a view, not a repeating roll. Faridabad living rooms with one long wall behind the sofa, shop shutters converted to studios in Ballabgarh, and kids’ rooms that need a landscape rather than a cartoon all use murals well.

Murals fail when the image is low-resolution, when an AC unit cuts a face in half, or when every wall is covered and the room has nowhere to rest the eye. We plan the crop around windows and the TV, and we will recommend leaving side walls in [texture](/wallpapers/textured/) or paint. If the artwork is yours, the job overlaps [customised printed wallpaper](/wallpapers/customised-printed/).

We do not invent famous-artist collaborations or “imported Italian mural” claims. What we can do is measure, proof, and install. Lead times depend on print. Prices stay off the page until the owner confirms a starting range.

## Panel planning

Murals are hung in sequence. A 12-foot wall is not “two rolls of 57 sq ft”; it is a set of panels cut to height. The [calculator](/tools/wallpaper-calculator/) still helps you understand area for quoting, but the order is made to the wall. Installation is skilled matching — see [wallpaper installation](/services/wallpaper-installation/).
