Bedrooms in Faridabad need rest more than drama. Wallpaper belongs mainly on the headboard wall: a soft [texture](/wallpapers/textured/), a muted [custom print](/wallpapers/customised-printed/), or a gentle mural that you can fall asleep under. High-contrast [3D](/wallpapers/3d/) brick behind the bed photographs well and can feel harsh at 6 a.m. — we will say if a sample is too busy.

Small bedrooms (common in NIT and older Sector 15–17 stock) need scale control. Large flowers and heavy damasks shrink the room. A vertical texture can lift a low ceiling visually; a panoramic mural needs enough wall. Read [how to choose wallpaper for a small bedroom](/blog/choose-wallpaper-small-bedroom/). Kids’ bedrooms are a separate brief: [kids’ room wallpaper](/wallpapers/kids-room/).

AC units sit on many Faridabad bedroom walls. We plan seams and motif away from the machine and the curtain stack. If the attached bathroom is humid, do not continue ordinary bedroom paper into that room — switch to [waterproof](/wallpapers/waterproof/) or tile.

We do not invent sleep-science or “Vaastu certified” wallpaper. We do consult, sample and install. Prices and night-counts stay off the site until confirmed.
