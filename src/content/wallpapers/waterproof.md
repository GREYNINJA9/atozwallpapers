Waterproof and highly washable wallpapers are the practical pick for Faridabad kitchens, utility rooms, powder rooms and bathrooms that are humid but not a tiled wet cubicle. Monsoon months and closed AC rooms both trap moisture. Paint in those spaces often spots; the wrong paper bubbles. The right vinyl or specialised waterproof product, hung on a sound wall, is easier to wipe.

We treat “waterproof” as a product claim to verify, not a slogan. Tell us if the wall is behind a hob, beside a shower, or only in a dry powder room. Direct water jets still belong on tile. Rising damp in older NIT and Ballabgarh houses needs a building fix before any decorative finish.

Compared with [textured](/wallpapers/textured/) living-room papers, waterproof ranges are usually smoother and more wipeable, with fewer deep grooves that trap grease. You can still choose a calm tile-look, marble-look or a custom print on a suitable substrate — see [customised printed](/wallpapers/customised-printed/). Kids’ bathrooms sometimes overlap with [kids’ room](/wallpapers/kids-room/) designs; durability comes first.

Installation includes checking the substrate. Fresh plaster must dry. We will not invent drying times. Starting prices are unpublished until confirmed. For area math, subtract windows and the hob opening in the [calculator](/tools/wallpaper-calculator/), then send the estimate for an exact quote.
