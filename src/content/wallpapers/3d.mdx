3D wallpaper is a feature-wall finish: bricks, stone, geometric relief or illusion panels that add depth without building actual masonry. In Faridabad homes it is most often used behind a sofa, a bed headboard or a reception desk — not as a wrap for the entire room.

The effect depends on viewing distance and light. A 10-foot living wall in Sector 19 behaves differently from a narrow NIT bedroom. Large faux-brick prints can overwhelm a compact room; finer geometric 3D often reads cleaner next to Indian apartment furniture. We help you match pattern scale to wall width so you are not left with a half-brick at the corner or a seam through a TV.

We do not claim every 3D product is washable or outdoor-rated. Ask for the collection name and care notes. [Waterproof wallpaper](/wallpapers/waterproof/) is the safer conversation for kitchens and baths. For a photographic scene rather than a relief look, compare [murals](/wallpapers/murals/) and [customised prints](/wallpapers/customised-printed/).

Installation still follows our consult → sample → install path. 3D and heavy textures may need extra paste and careful butting at internal corners. Confirm timeline and inclusions when you book; we will not publish invented day-counts or rupee starting prices until the owner confirms them.

## Choosing 3D for Faridabad light

West-facing rooms in Greater Faridabad get strong afternoon sun that can bleach some inks. North-facing Sector 15–17 rooms stay cooler in colour. If you send photos at two times of day, we can warn you when a high-contrast 3D brick will look busy. Pair with [living room](/wallpapers/living-room/) or [office](/wallpapers/office/) guidance if that is the space.
