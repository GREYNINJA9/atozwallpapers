Faridabad kitchens and bathrooms are humid for months at a time. Homeowners search for the “best waterproof wallpaper” and land on ranked lists that name brands we cannot verify for your wall. This guide is a decision method, not a paid podium.

## Waterproof vs washable vs “bathroom”

- **Washable:** you can wipe marks. Not a promise against standing water.
- **Waterproof / extra-washable vinyls:** better for steam and splashes on *dry, sound* walls.
- **Shower cubicle:** still tile (or a system designed as a wet-room). Wallpaper is not a replacement for a waterproof tanking membrane.

If a seller says “100% waterproof, hang in the shower”, be sceptical. We would rather refuse the wall.

## What to inspect in a Faridabad room

Look at the ceiling for black spots, the skirting for salt, and whether the exhaust actually runs. Old NIT flats and inner rooms in Ballabgarh houses often have cooler walls that sweat when the AC is on in the next room. Greater Faridabad apartments with no window in the powder room need ventilation more than they need a marble-look print.

Substrate comes first. Fresh plaster must dry. Painted walls need to be sound. We do not invent drying days — your mason and the weather decide.

## Specification questions to ask us

1. Is this wall a splash zone, steam zone, or only occasionally wiped?
2. What cleaning chemical does the household use?
3. Do you cook with a lot of oil on an open plan living-kitchen?
4. Can we sample the actual product, not a photo of a European spa?

## Aftercare

Wipe with a soft cloth. Avoid flooding seams. If a leak appears in the plumbing, stop decorating until that is fixed — paper will not save a wet wall.
