Paint is familiar. Wallpaper is a decision. Faridabad homeowners usually ask which is cheaper, which lasts through monsoon, and which looks less “builder flat”. This article explains how to compare them without fake rupee tables. We will not publish starting prices until the owner confirms numbers; your quote should always be for *your* walls.

## What you are actually buying

Paint cost is material plus labour plus primer, putty and often a second coat after the first looks patchy on old NIT plaster. Wallpaper cost is the paper (or custom print), adhesive, wastage (typically 10–15%), and skilled hanging. A feature wall of wallpaper can cost more per square foot than a coat of paint and still be the smaller invoice because you are not doing the whole house.

Where paint wins: ceilings, out-of-square utility walls, rooms you will change in a year, and any surface that is still damp. Where wallpaper wins: a living-room TV wall that looks empty after paint, a bedroom headboard that needs texture, a reception that needs a logo, or a mural that paint cannot imitate.

## Faridabad-specific wear

Monsoon humidity in closed rooms attacks both finishes if the wall is wet. Paint blisters; cheap paper peels. Neither is a waterproofing system. For kitchens and powder rooms, ask about washable or waterproof wallpaper — and still tile the shower. Greater Faridabad west sun fades some inks and some cheap paints equally; samples in your actual light beat Instagram.

Labour availability also differs. A painter may be easier to find on a Sunday; a careful wallpaper installer should not be rushed around AC units and patterned repeats. If a quote for wallpaper is “too cheap”, ask who is matching the pattern.

## How to compare on one wall

1. Measure width × height, subtract doors and windows.
2. Ask for a paint quote (including putty) for that wall only.
3. Run the [wallpaper calculator](/tools/wallpaper-calculator/) for rolls, then ask us for an exact quote (paper + install).
4. Decide how many years you want the look. Wallpaper you hate is expensive; paint you can redo is cheaper to reverse.
